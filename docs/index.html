Questions

Good to know.

Which formats are supported?

Filion currently supports GLB, the binary form of glTF.

How do the viewer controls work?

Drag to rotate, use two fingers to pan, and pinch or use the zoom control to move closer.

Why add scan folders?

Filion can collect GLB files from folders you choose and show them together on the home screen.

Where can I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub. Include your device, Android version, and what happened.
