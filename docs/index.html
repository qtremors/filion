Privacy

Your models stay on your device.

Filion does not declare Android's internet permission. It cannot upload your models, and it includes no advertising, analytics, telemetry, accounts, or cloud service.

Questions

Good to know.

Which formats are supported?

Filion currently supports GLB, the binary form of glTF.

Why choose scan folders?

Android lets you grant read access to specific folders instead of all storage. Filion remembers the folders you choose until you remove them in Settings.

Where can I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub. Include your device, Android version, and what happened.
